# Code of conduct

ArchAI is built by people from different places, backgrounds and levels of experience. To keep
it a good place to work together:

- Be respectful and patient. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good intent, ask before assuming, and help newcomers find their way.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or unwelcome personal or sexual attention, in any
  project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats).
- Respect people's time and privacy; do not share others' personal information.

If something goes wrong, email **mklkoklpp@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers
may remove comments, close threads or block people who do not follow this code.
