# ArchAI 0.2.2.21 — VR preview, bake locking and saved preparation

Imported meshes without normals could make Fast preview nearly black in the ambient-occlusion
pass. Preparation now generates the required normals without changing the board's shared asset
geometry. Completed baked scenes bypass preview AO/bloom so stored shading is not applied twice.

The baker now lights the two visible sides of double-sided imports independently. Inward-wound
roofs and terrain no longer use their underside's lighting for the visible top. Flat materials
retain face normals and exact duplicate faces are removed. White imported tree silhouettes
remain source geometry; diffuse vertex lighting still approximates glass, reflections and
cutout shadows. This update does not introduce path-traced texture-atlas baking.

The preparation workspace and camera navigation lock while baking, rendering or publishing.
Incoming model updates wait until the operation completes. Cancellation remains available for
baking and rendering. An edit made on the board during preparation is applied afterwards and
marks that saved snapshot outdated rather than changing or interrupting the active bake.

Completed baked GLB and 360 PNG outputs are saved separately per project and restored on reopening.
Failed writes and cancelled replacements preserve the previous completed output. Saved data is
local to this app installation; it is not embedded in exported `.AIR` files or synced to another
device. Already published links must be rebaked and published again to use the corrected lighting.
